Who We Are

Clothing is an agricultural act. We choose natural fibers to heal the soil and free our bodies.

Origin & Heritage

Crafted slowly between the foothills of Kathmandu and London streets.

Steering away from polyester and fast cycles.

Synthetic activewear traps moisture, sheds microplastics, and disconnects us from the ground. Jaivik is a response to that manufactured rush.

We work directly with Himalayan tailors and artisan communities, building durable, multi-purpose garments out of wild and organically farmed natural fibers that breathe with you in motion.

The Philosophy of Practicwear
“When you wear natural hemp, you are wearing an extension of the earth—durable enough for heavy movement, refined enough for daily living.”

No seasonal trends. No artificial discounts. Just pure material integrity, transparent bi-annual community reporting, and functional design built to last for years.

Rooted Ethics

Transparency isn’t a label. It’s a bi-annual commitment.
